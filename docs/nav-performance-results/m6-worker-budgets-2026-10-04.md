The 32MiB snapshot allowance bounds supported retained copied backing at reader start. It does not cap live generation arrays or temporary owner construction/reallocation, and it is checked after owner snapshot preparation. A retired copied snapshot may retain its backing until the owner replaces or reuses it; no second reader generation is published. Service storage is destroyed and its pin released before that replacement. Owner construction transients and full native route contexts are outside this reader budget and must be included in M7/M8 total-memory measurements.

The executable PE64 default thread stack reserve is 1,048,576 bytes with 4,096-byte initial commit. Standard runtime/OS thread state and stack storage are separate from copied-payload ceilings; this is the executable default, not a process-wide memory guarantee.

Geometry inside the supported storage envelope can still exhaust time/work. Such a query returns `resource_limited` with reason and empty corridor, rather than claiming unreachable. There is no unbounded synchronous escape inside this service. Face resolution, native funnel/refinement, waypoint records, collision proof and actor commit remain owner-only; this graph worker has no waypoint/trace buffer. M7 must connect pending/backoff states and measure/bound the native consumer before activation. M8 must calibrate optimized 64-agent load and default-enable separately.
